Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to common questions regarding visits, treatments, and procedures at Mohan Dental Care.

Do I need to book an appointment before visiting? expand_more

While we warmly welcome walk-in patients during routine clinic hours (9:00 AM – 8:00 PM), scheduling your slot in advance via phone (8839557607) or our online booking form ensures zero waiting time.

Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful at Mohan Dental Care? expand_more

Not at all. With modern local anesthesia, rotary endodontics, and precision digital RVG diagnostics, root canal procedures are virtually painless and designed to relieve existing toothache swiftly.

What are the qualifications of the doctor? expand_more

Dr. P.R. Rajwade completed his B.D.S. from the prestigious Government Dental College, Raipur. He is an officially verified practitioner registered with the Chhattisgarh Dental Council (Reg: CGDC/G/23/3989).

Are Sunday appointments available? expand_more

Yes, Sundays are reserved exclusively for pre-booked appointments and scheduled procedures to accommodate working professionals and families.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned? expand_more

Dental organizations universally recommend professional scaling and oral screening once every 6 months to prevent gum disease, tartar buildup, and premature enamel loss.
