## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a clinical presence that balances state-of-the-art dental precision with profound hospitality and gentleness. Tailored for a premier dental practice serving families, professionals, and generational residents in Surajpur, the aesthetic strips away the stark, anxiety-inducing sterility of conventional healthcare spaces in favor of calm, reassuring luxury.

The emotional core is defined by four pillars:
- **Quiet Reassurance:** Replacing dental apprehension with visual softness, structured calm, and unhurried whitespace.
- **Scientific Authority:** Grounded in deep architectural blues that convey uncompromised hygiene, institutional longevity, and professional mastery.
- **Warm Welcome:** Rooted in regional respect, introducing warm ivory canvas tones and delicate metallic restraint that reflect premium Indian hospitality.
- **Tactile Softness:** Generous radii, pillular indicators, and diffused light casting that simulate physical comfort and pain-free care.

The overarching aesthetic blends **Modern Clinical Restraint** with **Warm Editorial Minimalism**. Crisp typography, soft multi-layered surfaces, and micro-accents of brushed gold create a bespoke, private-clinic tier experience.

## Colors
The palette evokes clean clinical waters, architectural depth, and balanced warmth. It deliberately avoids harsh hospital cyans, high-intensity neon blues, or abrasive medical reds.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary (`#123B5D` - Deep Navy):** The structural anchor. Used for high-emphasis typographic elements, primary call-to-action buttons, high-priority navigation bars, and critical state framing. Communicates stability and deep professional experience.
- **Secondary (`#2E6F95` - Professional Blue):** The supportive clinical layer. Used for interactive states, secondary buttons, section category tags, active tab indicators, and icon fills.
- **Tertiary (`#55A6A6` - Soft Teal):** The restorative accent. Evoking gentle hygiene and healing, applied to progress indicators, appointment confirmation badges, checkmarks, and subtle decorative framing.
- **Background & Canvas (`#FAFBFC` - Warm White):** The dominant canvas layer. A soft, eye-resting white infused with warmth, eliminating the glare and coldness of pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Surface Secondary (`#F3F7F9` - Soft Ice Blue):** Used for elevated cards, nested input areas, subtle hover fills, table row alternations, and doctor profile backdrop panels.
- **Micro-Accent (`#D4AF37` - Brushed Gold):** A strictly rationed metallic accent honoring refined Indian craftsmanship. Restricted to micro-elements: VIP/consultation badges, star ratings, certificate highlights, and delicate keyline accents. Never applied as massive background washes.
- **Typography Tones:**
  - High Contrast (`#18303F` - Dark Navy): Primary headings, body copy, and active labels.
  - Medium Contrast (`#60727D` - Slate Navy): Supporting text, timestamps, captions, field placeholders, and helper text.

## Typography
The typography pairing reflects technical clarity paired with human accessibility:

- **Headlines & Titles (Plus Jakarta Sans):** The geometric yet warm curves of Plus Jakarta Sans lend a modern, approachable face to clinical headings. Its geometric aperture maintains maximum legibility while avoiding technical harshness. Titles employ tight negative tracking to maintain editorial cohesion across larger displays.
- **Body & Long-form Content (Inter):** Applied across all body paragraphs, patient medical forms, consent documentation, and service breakdowns. Inter provides neutral, unencumbered legibility at text sizes, preventing eye fatigue during extensive reading.
- **Functional Labels & Interactive UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Buttons, tabs, step flows, and metadata badges use medium-to-semibold weights of Plus Jakarta Sans with gentle positive tracking for fast micro-scanning.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an open, air-filled column system designed to invoke a calm breathing pace. Generous padding around elements reflects physical cleanliness and uncrowded, private clinic suites.

### Layout Grid & Responsive Rules
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with fixed max-width container of 1280px. Columns sit on a `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutter and `3rem` (`48px`) exterior margins. Layouts preserve generous white margins around cards and editorial sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Two-column service and doctor grids collapse gracefully into stacked structures.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins. Spacing between major clinical modules compresses to maintain continuous narrative momentum without feeling dense.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Section vertical spacing respects a consistent rhythm: `4rem` to `6rem` on desktop, scaling to `2.5rem` to `3.5rem` on mobile devices.
- Internal component distances strictly employ the `space-*` scale to maintain consistent density throughout dashboards, appointment forms, and informational panels.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation conveys clinical hygiene, tactile layering, and quiet serenity. The system rejects deep black dropshadows, noisy textures, and stark brutalist borders. Depth is articulated through three harmonized mechanisms:

### Tonal Stratification
Depth is created primarily through natural surface progression:
- **Base Canvas:** `#FAFBFC` (Warm White).
- **Secondary Surfaces:** `#F3F7F9` (Soft Ice Blue) for background sections, calendar containers, and feature showcases.
- **Elevated Canvas:** Pure `#FFFFFF` for interactive cards, dialogs, and navigation floating above `#F3F7F9` or `#FAFBFC`.

### Diffused Tinted Shadows
Shadows simulate soft, overhead surgical ambient light diffused through frosted lenses:
- **Subtle Surface (`shadow-sm`):** `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.03)` — used for persistent cards, input controls, and chip selectors.
- **Floating Element (`shadow-md`):** `0px 12px 28px -6px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.03)` — used for active booking drawers, floating navigation bars, and appointment summary toasts.
- **Lifted Interactive (`shadow-lg`):** `0px 24px 44px -10px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.12), 0px 8px 18px -4px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.04)` — reserved for dialogs, modal booking workflows, and elevated treatment previews.

### Low-Contrast Structural Outlines
All cards and interactive surfaces feature a fine 1px ghost boundary: `border: 1px solid rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.07)`. This ensures clear geometric definition even in bright lighting conditions without producing heavy visual contrast.

## Shapes
The design system applies a generous, organic radius philosophy that feels soft to the human touch, avoiding cold angles while maintaining surgical order.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`). Provides smooth, cradling geometry for doctor cards, service dossiers, and treatment summaries.
- **Internal Modules & Nested Panels:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`). Used for calendar pickers, slot selectors, and diagnostic detail boxes.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, and Action Fields:** Set to a calibrated `12px` radius (`0.75rem`), balancing modern softness with architectural precision.
- **Chips, Badges, and Micro-Pills:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) to maintain distinct separation from structured cards and fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#123B5D`) fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` text. Height of `48px`, padding `0 24px`, border-radius `12px`. Hover state smoothly transitions to `#2E6F95` with a subtle elevation increase (`shadow-sm`).
- **Secondary Action:** Ghost surface with Soft Ice Blue background (`#F3F7F9`), Deep Navy text, and a fine 1px border in `rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.12)`. Hover shifts background to pure `#FFFFFF` with `rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.2)` outline.
- **Gold Accent (VIP / Urgent Consultation):** Gold contour (`1px solid #D4AF37`) on an ultra-pale gold tinted background (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`), text in Dark Navy (`#18303F`). Used sparingly for direct WhatsApp or emergency specialist triage.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background is pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#FAFBFC` canvas, or `#F3F7F9` resting on pure `#FFFFFF` cards.
- Height is `48px` with `12px` roundedness and `1px solid rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.14)`.
- Typography is Inter 15px with Dark Navy text and Slate Navy (`#60727D`) placeholder text.
- Focus state activates an outline transition: `1.5px solid #2E6F95` accompanied by an ambient focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(85, 166, 166, 0.18)`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Constructed using `20px` to `24px` corner radii with a pure white `#FFFFFF` surface and `1px solid rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.06)` outline.
- Cards maintain interior padding of `space-lg` (`24px`).
- Treatment tiles feature a Soft Teal (`#55A6A6`) micro-indicator or icon container tinted at `12%` opacity with matching icon stroke.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Fully rounded pills (`height: 32px`, `padding: 0 14px`).
- **Available Slots / Open:** Soft Teal tint (`rgba(85, 166, 166, 0.12)`) with `#226C6C` text and a `6px` solid dot.
- **Specialty / Category Chips:** Soft Ice Blue (`#F3F7F9`) background with Deep Navy (`#123B5D`) text. Active state inverts to `#123B5D` fill with white text.
- **Accreditation / Elite Badge:** Pale Gold tint (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`) with `#8C711C` text and micro gold icon.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- `20px` dimensional footprint with `6px` roundedness for checkboxes and complete roundness for radio elements.
- Inactive state: `1.5px solid rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.22)` with pure white core.
- Active state: `#123B5D` fill hosting a sharp white checkmark or inner dot, surrounded by an ambient Teal micro-glow (`rgba(85, 166, 166, 0.2)`).

### Specialized Clinical Modules
- **Doctor / Specialist Credentials Card:** Combines portrait imagery with rounded-xl containers, featuring an anchored certification badge trimmed with a 1px `#D4AF37` hairline.
- **Interactive Appointment Booking Matrix:** Clean calendar grid utilizing Soft Ice Blue slot blocks that highlight to Deep Navy upon patient selection.
- **Before / After Clinical Slider:** Framed in 20px radii, using low-contrast `#18303F` handle bars and Soft Teal demarcation lines.
